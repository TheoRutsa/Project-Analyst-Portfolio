# Project Success Criteria

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Phase:** Project Initiation  
**Document:** Project Success Criteria  

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define how the success of the Maji Ndogo Water Services Project will be measured.

The project will be considered successful when it demonstrates the ability to transform a defined business problem into a validated, data-driven decision-support solution while applying structured project-management practices throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## 2. Overall Success Definition

The project will be considered successful when:

> **Available Maji Ndogo water-services data is transformed into a validated, reproducible and decision-oriented Business Intelligence solution that addresses defined business questions and demonstrates effective project management from initiation through closure.**
