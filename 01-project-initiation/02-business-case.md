# Business Case

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Delivery Approach:** Structured End-to-End Project Lifecycle

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Maji Ndogo Water Services Project is a data-driven decision-support initiative focused on analysing water access, water quality, service accessibility, infrastructure requirements and community impact.

The project integrates **Project Management, Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** into a single delivery lifecycle.

The purpose is to transform available operational datasets into validated insights that can support the identification of priority areas, infrastructure requirements and potential intervention priorities.

The project combines structured project governance with technical data analysis to demonstrate how a Project Manager with data capabilities can move from an identified business problem to an evidence-based decision-support solution.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Water-service challenges cannot be understood through a single metric.

A location may experience:

- High population dependence on a water source
- Poor water-quality results
- Long waiting times
- Limited access to suitable infrastructure
- Significant infrastructure requirements
- Different levels of community impact

Analysing these factors independently can make it difficult to determine where attention should be prioritised.

The business problem is therefore:

> **How can available water-service data be transformed into a structured, validated and decision-oriented framework that identifies the most significant service challenges and supports prioritisation of potential interventions?**

---

## 3. Business Need

A structured analytical approach is required to:

1. Consolidate information from multiple datasets.
2. Establish reliable and measurable KPIs.
3. Identify patterns in water quality and accessibility.
4. Quantify population exposure to service challenges.
5. Assess waiting-time and queue burdens.
6. Evaluate infrastructure requirements and estimated investment.
7. Compare conditions across geographic areas.
8. Develop a prioritisation framework.
9. Present findings through executive-level reporting.
10. Create an interactive Business Intelligence solution.
11. Establish a foundation for future automation and AI-assisted analysis.

## 7. Key Business Questions

The project is designed to answer the following questions.

### Water Quality

- Where are water-quality issues most prevalent?
- What proportion of assessed sources are contaminated?
- What types of contamination are represented?

### Water Access

- Which water sources serve the largest populations?
- Which locations experience greater access burdens?
- How do conditions differ between rural and urban locations?

### Queue & Service Access

- Which water sources experience the longest queues?
- What proportion of visits involve non-zero waiting times?
- Where is the greatest service-access burden observed?

### Infrastructure

- Which areas have the greatest infrastructure requirements?
- Which intervention types require the greatest estimated investment?
- How are infrastructure requirements distributed geographically?

### Prioritisation

- Which areas demonstrate the greatest combination of risk and population exposure?
- Which factors contribute most strongly to the prioritisation score?
- Where could further investigation or intervention potentially provide the greatest benefit?

The portfolio project is based on the assumption that:

- The supplied datasets represent the available analytical source material.

## 12. Key Constraints

The project is subject to several constraints.

### Data Constraints

The analysis is limited to the variables and observations contained within the supplied datasets.

### Historical Context

The source material does not necessarily provide complete information about an actual historical project-management lifecycle.

### Analytical Limitations

The analysis identifies relationships and patterns within the available data but does not establish causality unless specifically supported by the evidence.

### Portfolio Context

Project-management documents that are not directly evidenced by the source material are reconstructed as part of this portfolio case study.
