# Stakeholder Analysis

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Phase:** Project Planning  
**Document:** Stakeholder Analysis  

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to determine how each stakeholder group should be engaged throughout the Maji Ndogo Water Services Project.

The analysis considers:

- Stakeholder influence
- Level of interest
- Project impact
- Decision-making authority
- Information requirements
- Engagement needs
- Potential risks

The output will inform the project's:

- Requirements management
- RACI matrix
- Communication plan
- UAT strategy
- Risk management
- Governance structure

---

# 2. Stakeholder Assessment Model

Stakeholders are assessed using two dimensions.

### Influence

The degree to which a stakeholder can affect:

- Project scope
- Requirements
- Resources
- Decisions
- Deliverables
- Acceptance
- Project outcomes

### Interest

The degree to which a stakeholder is affected by or interested in:

- Project progress
- Analytical findings
- Dashboard outputs
- Recommendations
- Business outcomes

### Keep Informed

> Stakeholder classifications represent the proposed governance structure for this portfolio case study and should be reviewed if project conditions or requirements change.

# 6. Stakeholder Prioritisation

Stakeholder prioritisation is based on:

### High Influence + High Interest

These stakeholders require the greatest level of engagement because they can materially affect project delivery and are directly invested in outcomes.

**Strategy:** Manage Closely

### High Influence + Lower Interest

These stakeholders need sufficient information to maintain confidence while avoiding unnecessary operational detail.

**Strategy:** Keep Satisfied

### Lower Influence + High Interest

These stakeholders should receive regular information and opportunities to provide feedback.

**Strategy:** Keep Informed

### Lower Influence + Lower Interest

These stakeholders require limited but appropriate monitoring.

**Strategy:** Monitor

# 7. Stakeholder Engagement Strategy

| Stakeholder Category | Engagement Approach | Information Level | Typical Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manage Closely | Active collaboration and decision involvement | Detailed | Weekly / Milestone |
| Keep Satisfied | Executive and exception reporting | High-level | Milestone / As Required |
| Keep Informed | Progress updates and review sessions | Relevant detail | Bi-weekly / Milestone |
| Monitor | Limited communication | Minimal | As Required |

Actual communication frequency will be refined in the Communication Plan.
