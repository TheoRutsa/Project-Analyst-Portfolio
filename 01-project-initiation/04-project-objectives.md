# Project Objectives

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Delivery Approach:** Structured End-to-End Project Lifecycle

---

## 1. Objective Overview

The primary objective of the Maji Ndogo Water Services Project is to transform available water-services data into a structured, validated and decision-oriented analytical solution.

The project combines:

- Project Management
- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence
- Data Quality Management
- Decision Support
- AI & Automation

The objectives have been designed to provide a clear connection between the identified business problem, project deliverables, analytical outputs and expected outcomes.

---

## 2. Primary Project Objective

> **To develop a validated data-driven decision-support solution that analyses water access, water quality, population exposure, service accessibility and infrastructure requirements, and presents the resulting insights through reproducible KPIs, analytical models, interactive Business Intelligence and executive reporting.**

---

## 3. Strategic Objectives

### Objective 1 — Establish a Structured Project Framework

Define and document the project from initiation through closure using a structured project-management lifecycle.

The project framework will establish:

- Project purpose
- Business case
- Problem statement
- Objectives
- Scope
- Stakeholders
- Requirements
- Risks
- Deliverables
- Success criteria
- Governance

**Expected outcome:**  
A clearly documented project structure that provides traceability across the complete project lifecycle.

---

### Objective 2 — Establish Data Understanding & Quality

Develop a clear understanding of the available Maji Ndogo datasets and assess their suitability for analysis.

Activities will include:

- Identifying available datasets.
- Reviewing fields and data types.
- Developing a data dictionary.
- Identifying missing values.
- Identifying duplicate records.
- Identifying inconsistent values.
- Assessing potential data-quality issues.
- Documenting data limitations.

**Expected outcome:**  
A documented and quality-assessed analytical data foundation.

---

### Objective 3 — Develop a Reproducible Analytical Framework

Create a structured analytical process that allows key results to be reproduced from the underlying data.

The framework will include:

- Data preparation.
- Data transformation.
- Analytical calculations.
- KPI definitions.
- SQL analysis.
- Excel / Power Query analysis.
- Validation procedures.
- Analytical documentation.

**Expected outcome:**  
A transparent analytical process where key results can be traced back to source data and calculation logic.

---

### Objective 4 — Analyse Water-Service Conditions

Analyse the major dimensions of water-service performance contained within the available datasets.

The analysis will investigate:

- Water quality.
- Water access.
- Population exposure.
- Queue and waiting times.
- Infrastructure requirements.
- Estimated investment requirements.
- Geographic differences.
- Community-level impact.

**Expected outcome:**  
A comprehensive analytical view of water-service conditions across the available data.

---

### Objective 5 — Develop a KPI Framework

Establish measurable and clearly defined KPIs that support consistent analysis and comparison.

Each KPI should include:

| KPI Attribute | Requirement |
|---|---|
| KPI Name | Clearly defined |
| Business Question | What the KPI answers |
| Definition | What is being measured |
| Calculation | Reproducible formula |
| Data Source | Source field(s) |
| Unit | Percentage, count, time, currency or other relevant unit |
| Interpretation | What a high/low result means |
| Validation | Method used to confirm the result |

**Expected outcome:**  
A validated KPI framework that provides consistent measures for the Business Intelligence solution.

---

## 4. Analytical Objectives

### 4.1 Water Quality Analysis

Determine the distribution and characteristics of water-quality conditions within the available data.

The analysis will seek to determine:

- The prevalence of identified quality concerns.
- Geographic distribution of quality concerns.
- Population exposure associated with affected sources.
- Differences between source types where applicable.

---

### 4.2 Water Access Analysis

Assess how populations interact with available water sources.

The analysis will investigate:

- Population served by sources.
- Source distribution.
- Access patterns.
- Geographic differences in access.
- Potential areas of higher population dependence.

---

### 4.3 Queue & Waiting-Time Analysis

Assess the service burden associated with waiting and queue conditions.

The analysis will investigate:

- Average waiting times.
- Distribution of waiting times.
- Locations with higher waiting times.
- Potential relationships between demand and waiting conditions.

---

### 4.4 Infrastructure Analysis

Assess infrastructure requirements represented within the available data.

The analysis will investigate:

- Infrastructure requirements.
- Types of infrastructure required.
- Geographic distribution.
- Population potentially affected.
- Estimated investment requirements.

---

### 4.5 Geographic Analysis

Identify differences in water-service conditions across geographic areas.

The analysis will compare relevant indicators by available geographic dimensions.

**Expected outcome:**  
A geographic view that helps identify areas demonstrating different combinations of service conditions.

---

## 5. Business Intelligence Objectives

### Objective 6 — Develop an Integrated BI Solution

Transform validated analytical outputs into an interactive Business Intelligence solution.

The solution should provide:

- Executive overview.
- KPI cards.
- Geographic analysis.
- Water-quality analysis.
- Water-access analysis.
- Queue and waiting-time analysis.
- Infrastructure analysis.
- Investment analysis.
- Prioritisation insights.
- Interactive filtering.

**Expected outcome:**  
A Business Intelligence dashboard that allows users to explore the data and identify meaningful patterns.
