# Stakeholder Register

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Delivery Approach:** Structured End-to-End Project Lifecycle  
**Phase:** Project Planning  
**Document:** Stakeholder Register  

---

## 1. Purpose

The Stakeholder Register identifies the stakeholder groups that may influence, contribute to, use or be affected by the Maji Ndogo Water Services Project.

Stakeholder management is important because the project combines:

- Project Management
- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence
- Decision Support
- AI & Automation

The register provides the foundation for:

- Stakeholder analysis
- Engagement planning
- Requirements gathering
- Communication planning
- RACI development
- Requirements traceability
- User acceptance testing

---

## 2. Stakeholder Context

The project is structured as a professional portfolio case study using the available Maji Ndogo water-services data.

The source material does not establish a complete historical stakeholder structure for an actual implementation programme.

Therefore, the stakeholder groups below represent a **proposed project governance and engagement framework** designed around the business problem, analytical requirements and intended decision-support solution.

They should not be interpreted as confirmed historical stakeholders unless independently supported by project source material.
