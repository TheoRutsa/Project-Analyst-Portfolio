# Business Case

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Delivery Approach:** Structured End-to-End Project Lifecycle  
**Project Status:** Portfolio Case Study

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The Maji Ndogo Water Services Project is an end-to-end data-driven decision-support initiative focused on analysing water access, water quality, service accessibility, infrastructure requirements and community impact.

The project integrates **Project Management, Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** into a single project lifecycle.

The purpose is to transform available operational data into validated insights that can support the identification of service challenges, potential priority areas and infrastructure requirements.

The project demonstrates the ability to move from a business problem through structured project planning, data preparation, analytical modelling, Business Intelligence development, validation and executive reporting.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Water-service challenges are influenced by multiple interconnected factors.

These may include:

- Water-source availability
- Population dependence on water sources
- Water-quality conditions
- Queue and waiting times
- Infrastructure requirements
- Geographic differences
- Estimated investment requirements
- Community-level impact

Analysing these factors independently can make it difficult to develop a consolidated view of service conditions or establish which areas may warrant greater attention.

The business problem is therefore:

> **How can available water-services data be transformed into a structured, validated and decision-oriented analytical solution that identifies significant service challenges and supports evidence-based prioritisation?**

---

## 3. Business Need

A structured analytical solution is required to:

1. Consolidate relevant water-service information.
2. Establish reliable and measurable KPIs.
3. Assess water-quality conditions.
4. Analyse water access and population exposure.
5. Evaluate queue and waiting-time patterns.
6. Assess infrastructure requirements.
7. Analyse estimated investment requirements.
8. Identify geographic differences.
9. Develop a prioritisation framework.
10. Present findings through Business Intelligence.
11. Produce executive-level reporting.
12. Establish opportunities for AI-assisted automation.
