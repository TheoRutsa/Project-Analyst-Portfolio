# Maji Ndogo Water Services
## End-to-End Project Management & Data Analytics Case Study

**Project Management | Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | AI-Assisted Analysis**

## Project Overview

The **Maji Ndogo Water Services Project** is an end-to-end portfolio case study combining **Project Management, Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** to investigate water access, water quality, infrastructure requirements and community impact.

The project demonstrates how a structured project lifecycle can be used to transform raw operational data into validated insights, executive reporting and data-driven intervention priorities.

The project is structured from **initiation through planning, execution, monitoring and control, analysis, quality assurance and closure**, with an additional future-state focus on AI-assisted automation.

**Note:** This repository presents a structured project-management reconstruction around the supplied Maji Ndogo dataset and analytical work. Project-management artefacts that are not directly evidenced by the source data are presented as proposed/reconstructed portfolio artefacts rather than claims of historical project events.

# Business Problem

Access to reliable and safe water is influenced by multiple interconnected factors, including:

- Availability and type of water sources
- Population dependent on each source
- Water-quality conditions
- Waiting and queue times
- Geographic differences
- Infrastructure requirements
- Community-level impacts
- Investment requirements

The challenge is therefore not simply to analyse individual datasets, but to integrate these factors into a **decision-support framework** that can help identify areas requiring attention and prioritise potential interventions.

# Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to develop a **data-driven decision-support solution** that:

1. Assesses water access and service availability.
2. Evaluates water-quality conditions.
3. Measures population exposure and access burdens.
4. Analyses queue and waiting-time patterns.
5. Assesses infrastructure requirements and estimated investment.
6. Identifies geographic areas requiring prioritisation.
7. Communicates findings through executive-level reporting and interactive visualisation.
8. Establishes a foundation for future AI-assisted analytical automation.
