# Project Scope

## Maji Ndogo Water Services Project

**Project Type:** Data-Driven Decision Support & Business Intelligence  
**Portfolio Focus:** Project Management + Data Analytics + Business Intelligence  
**Delivery Approach:** Structured End-to-End Project Lifecycle  
**Document:** Project Scope  
**Phase:** Project Initiation  

---

## 1. Scope Overview

The Maji Ndogo Water Services Project will develop an end-to-end, data-driven decision-support solution using the available Maji Ndogo water-services data.

The project combines:

- Project Management
- Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence
- Data Quality Management
- Decision Support
- AI & Automation

The scope establishes the boundaries within which the project will be planned, executed, analysed, validated and closed.

The project will move from:

Business Problem
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
Business Intelligence
      ↓
Validation


## 2. Scope Statement

The project will analyse the available Maji Ndogo water-services data to assess water access, water quality, population exposure, waiting-time conditions, infrastructure requirements and related indicators. The project will transform the available data into validated analytical outputs, reproducible KPIs, an interactive Business Intelligence solution, a prioritisation framework and executive-level decision support.

The project will also document the complete project-management lifecycle and identify opportunities to improve repetitive analytical and reporting activities through AI and automation.

12. Out of Scope
The following activities are explicitly outside the scope of this project.

12.1 Physical Infrastructure Implementation
The project will not:
Construct infrastructure.
Repair infrastructure.
Install water systems.
Implement physical interventions.

12.2 Procurement
The project will not manage:
Supplier procurement
Tender processes
Contractor selection
Equipment purchasing
Procurement contracts

12.3 Funding Approval
The project will not:
Approve budgets.
Allocate funding.
Secure financing.
Authorise infrastructure expenditure.

12.4 Field Data Collection
The project will not conduct:
New surveys
Physical inspections
Water-quality laboratory testing
Community interviews
New field measurements

The analysis is based on the available source data.

12.5 Policy Implementation
The project will not directly implement:
Government policy
Municipal policy
Water-service regulations
Infrastructure programmes

12.6 Operational Management
The project will not assume responsibility for:
Managing water facilities
Managing water-service operations
Managing field teams
Managing infrastructure maintenance

12.7 Final Investment Decisions
The project will provide analytical decision support but will not make final investment decisions.

17. Scope Quality Principle
The project will follow the principle:
Do not build an analytical output simply because it is technically possible. Build it because it addresses a defined business or analytical requirement.
This principle will help prevent:
Unnecessary dashboard features
Unsupported KPIs
Uncontrolled scope expansion
Analysis without a defined business purpose

18. Scope Summary
The project scope encompasses the complete transformation of available water-services data into a validated decision-support solution.
The core scope can be summarised as:
Define → Understand → Prepare → Analyse → Measure → Visualise → Validate → Prioritise → Communicate → Close
The project will demonstrate how structured project management can be combined with data analytics and Business Intelligence to address a complex operational problem.
