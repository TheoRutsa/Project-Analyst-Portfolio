## 1. Project Purpose

The purpose of the Maji Ndogo Water Services Project is to develop a structured, data-driven decision-support solution for analysing water access, water quality, service accessibility, infrastructure requirements and community impact.

The project combines **Project Management, Data Analytics and Business Intelligence** into a single end-to-end delivery lifecycle.

The project demonstrates how a complex business problem can be translated into:

**Business Requirements → Data → Analysis → KPIs → Business Intelligence → Validation → Decision Support**

The final solution is intended to provide a clear evidence base for identifying patterns, assessing potential priorities and communicating findings to decision makers.

---

## 2. Background

The Maji Ndogo dataset contains information relating to water services and provides an opportunity to investigate multiple dimensions of water access and service delivery.

The analytical challenge extends beyond simply calculating individual statistics.

A meaningful decision-support solution requires the integration of:

- Water-source information
- Population information
- Water-quality information
- Queue and waiting-time information
- Infrastructure requirements
- Geographic information
- Estimated investment requirements

The project therefore uses a structured project lifecycle to transform the available data into a coherent analytical and Business Intelligence solution.

---

## 3. Business Problem

Water-service conditions can vary significantly between locations and water sources.

Challenges may include:

- Poor water quality
- High population dependence on individual sources
- Long waiting and queue times
- Limited or inadequate infrastructure
- Significant infrastructure requirements
- Unequal service conditions between geographic areas

When these factors are analysed independently, it becomes difficult to determine which locations or service challenges should receive greater attention.

The project therefore seeks to answer:

> **How can available water-service data be transformed into a validated, structured and decision-oriented framework that identifies significant service challenges and supports evidence-based prioritisation?**

---
